### 🗣️ Your Dialogue / Pitch
"Sir, maine recently 2025 ka **'Health-LLM'** paper study kiya. Unka disease prediction model bohat acha hai, lekin usme ek major practical issue hai: **The Black-Box Problem**. Standard models (like GPT or Llama) prediction toh de dete hain (e.g., '89% chance of Diabetes'), par doctors aisi prediction pe blindly trust nahi kar sakte jab tak unhe AI ka internal logic na pata ho. 

Is gap ko fill karne ke liye, maine apni research mein latest **DeepSeek-R1** reasoning model ko pipeline mein integrate kiya. Ab mera model final prediction dene se pehle ek `<think>` trace generate karta hai jo clinical guidelines (jaise ADA rules) se symptoms ko map karta hai. Yeh approach AI ko **Explainable (XAI)** banati hai, jo real-world healthcare mein bohat zaroori hai."

---

### 🔑 Key Terms to Drop in Conversation
* **Black-Box AI:** AI that gives an answer without showing its logic.
* **Explainable AI (XAI):** AI that shows its thought process (highly valued in medical fields).
* **Clinical Chain of Thought:** The step-by-step medical reasoning generated by DeepSeek-R1.
